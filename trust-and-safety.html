Trust & Safety

Trust is not a feature. It is the foundation.

Emotional trust does not emerge accidentally. The pace, the incentives, the expectations and the environment itself all shape the experience people have with one another. Converge was designed with that in mind from the start.

I. Privacy

Room to get to know someone, without the scatter.

Early conversations remain within the platform, so there is no immediate pressure to exchange phone numbers, social media accounts or other contact details.

Many people know the strange exhaustion of modern dating — the rapid migration across texts, Instagram and disappearing chats that blur together over time. Converge slows that down deliberately.

II. Your Information

What we hold, and what we never do with it.

Never sold

User information is not sold, rented or treated as a commodity. Membership fees fund the platform — nothing else.

Your answers stay private

Other members never see your application answers. They inform introductions and nothing more.

Declined means deleted

Applications that are not accepted are deleted within 30 days of the decision.

Verified identity

Every member is identity-verified before admission. Anonymity is not available within the platform.

III. Emotional Accountability

Closure is treated as part of emotional maturity — not an optional courtesy.

No system can eliminate disappointment, incompatibility or human complexity. But Converge is designed to encourage accountability rather than disposability. When a connection is not progressing, members say so — directly and respectfully — rather than disappearing into ambiguity.

Community standards exist not to police ordinary human interaction, but to protect the integrity of the experience — an environment where people feel comfortable showing up honestly and approaching one another with mutual respect.

Plainly stated

What verification covers

Converge verifies each member's identity. We do not conduct criminal background checks. As with meeting anyone new, take the same care you would anywhere — especially when you first meet in person.

If something feels wrong

Tell us directly

Concerns about another member's conduct go to a person, not a queue. Every report is read and followed up.
