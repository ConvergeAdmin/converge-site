Our Framework

Designed for depth, not volume.

Modern dating platforms are built around immediacy. Conversations begin quickly, overlap constantly, and often disappear before any real understanding has had time to emerge.

Converge was designed differently from the beginning: curated cohorts, a limited number of introductions, and a pace that leaves room for genuine exploration.

Stages 01 – 03

Voice

An introduction and two audio-only calls. You listen before you look.

Stage 04

Sight

Video opens only when both people choose to continue.

Stages 05 – 06

Commitment

One connection, full attention, and the move into real-world courtship.

The Six Stages

Each stage deepens clarity before it deepens investment.

01

Curated Introduction

Converge reviews compatibility across values, long-term life goals, communication patterns and emotional temperament — not surface-level preference. Each cohort member then receives up to three introductions.

Format — Introduction

Up to three per cohort

02

The First Call

Thirty minutes, audio only, guided by structured prompts shared in advance. The conversation is intentional, not improvisational.

Format — Audio

30 minutes · Guided

03

The Second Call

Sixty minutes, still audio only, and unguided. By now there is enough shared context to go further — and carrying the conversation yourselves is a useful signal in its own right.

Format — Audio

60 minutes · Unguided

04

Open Exploration

Both of you have chosen to continue, and video unlocks for the first time. Connections remain concurrent — no one is asked to close anything yet. That progression is earned, not assumed.

Format — Video

Up to 60 minutes · Self-directed

05

Full Attention

A mutual, active choice. Both people close every other connection — with an honest message to each — and give this one their full attention. Contact details can now be exchanged. This is where real-world courtship begins.

Format — Open

One connection only

06

Real-World Integration

Optional relationship modules, first-meeting guidance and resources for the move from courtship to partnership. Converge does not prescribe outcomes — it equips members to reach them deliberately.

Format — Optional

Guidance and resources

Why Voice First

Without the distraction of visual presentation, people tend to listen more carefully — and speak more honestly.

Early conversations also stay within the platform. That creates distance from the performative dynamics that shape so much of online interaction: rapid-fire texting, social media migration, and the pressure to define a connection before it has had time to breathe.

Structured, but not rigid.

Thoughtful starting conditions

Chemistry will always be unpredictable, and no framework can guarantee connection. The aim is to help people stay open long enough for depth and curiosity to develop.

Every pace is valid

Not every connection becomes romantic. Some conversations begin slowly and develop surprising depth; others bring clarity quickly. The framework makes room for both.

Respectful closure

When a connection does not progress, members say so directly and graciously. Emotional maturity is not treated as an ideal trait here. It is built into the structure itself.

A process that feels less rushed, less disposable, and more capable of sustaining genuine presence from beginning to end.
