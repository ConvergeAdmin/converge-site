IV. Our Standards

This is a managed environment.

Converge maintains the integrity of the process through clear, consistently enforced standards. Membership is funded by members — never by advertising, and never by selling your data.

Identity Verification

Every member is identity-verified prior to admission. Anonymity is not available within the platform.

Early-Stage Conduct

Explicit sexual communication during the First and Second Call stages results in immediate, permanent removal.

Off-Platform Contact

Requesting handles, numbers or outside contact during early call stages is prohibited and enforced.

Connection Limits

A maximum of three active connections per cohort. The limit is enforced, not optional.

Full Attention

Entering Full Attention requires closing all other connections first. A condition of the stage, not a preference.

Direct Endings

Endings are communicated directly and respectfully. Ghosting is treated as a conduct violation.

Common Questions

Asked and answered.

Is this a dating app?

No. Converge is a structured courtship system. There is no feed to scroll and no algorithm to game. Introductions are curated, not generated.

Why limit connections to three?

Because divided attention produces diluted investment. Three is enough for genuine evaluation without the shallow parallelism of most platforms.

Why audio-only for the first two calls?

Visual presentation triggers quick judgements that short-circuit genuine assessment. By the time video arrives, a real foundation is already there.

Why annual membership?

Monthly billing rewards platforms for keeping you subscribed. An annual commitment aligns our incentives with yours: we succeed when you find what you came for.

Is Converge inclusive?

Converge is open to all adults, regardless of gender, orientation, ethnicity, religion or background, who are seeking a serious long-term partnership. The one non-negotiable is intent.

Where is Converge available?

Converge is currently open to residents of the United States and Canada. International expansion is planned; anyone may join the waitlist and will be notified when Converge reaches their area.

Is relocation required?

No. Geography is considered during introductions, but early courtship proceeds remotely. Whether relocation matters is a conversation for the two people involved.
