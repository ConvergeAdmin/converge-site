The System

The Converge
Courtship Framework

Six deliberate stages. Each one designed to deepen clarity before deepening investment.

01
Introduction

Curated Introduction

Converge reviews compatibility across values, life direction, and context — not surface-level preference. Once compatibility is assessed, each cohort member receives up to three introductions.

02
Audio · 30 minutes

The First Call

A thirty-minute audio-only call. Guided by structured prompts provided in advance. Video is not available at this stage — voice carries what a photograph cannot. The conversation is intentional, not improvisational.

03
Audio · 60 minutes

The Second Call

A sixty-minute continuation, audio-only. By this point, there is enough shared context to go further. This call is unguided. Members are expected to carry the conversation themselves, which is a useful signal in its own right.

04
Video · 60 minutes

Open Exploration

Both parties have chosen to continue. Video unlocks for the first time. Connections remain concurrent at this stage — no one is asked to close anything yet. This is an opportunity to see one another, deepen what the audio calls began, and move toward a decision with greater clarity. Calls are self-directed and capped at sixty minutes.

05
One Connection

Full Attention

A mutual, active choice. Both people have decided to close all other connections and give this one their full attention. Format is entirely open — scheduled calls, video, or whatever feels natural. At this stage, members are encouraged to exchange contact information and move beyond the platform. This is the beginning of real-world courtship.

06
Optional

Real-World Integration

Optional relationship modules, first-meeting guidance, and structured resources for couples navigating the transition from courtship to partnership. Converge does not prescribe outcomes — it equips members to reach them deliberately.

Our Standards

This is a managed
environment.

Converge maintains the integrity of the process through clear, consistently enforced standards. Membership is a privilege, not a right.

Identity Verification

All members are identity-verified prior to admission. Anonymity is not available within the platform.

Early-Stage Conduct

Explicit sexual communication during the First and Second Call stages results in immediate and permanent removal.

Off-Platform Contact

Requesting social handles, phone numbers, or off-platform contact during early call stages is prohibited and enforceable.

Connection Limits

Members may hold a maximum of three active connections at any time. This limit is enforced, not optional.

Full Attention

Entering Full Attention mode requires closing all other active connections before proceeding. This is a condition of the stage, not a preference.

Direct Endings

Members are expected to communicate endings directly and respectfully. Ghosting is treated as a conduct violation.

Why Converge Exists

Attention is abundant.
Alignment is not.

"Modern platforms were built to reward engagement. We built Converge to reward intention."

The dominant logic of online dating is one of volume: more profiles, more swipes, more messages. The underlying assumption is that exposure leads to connection. For most people seeking a genuine partnership, this assumption has proven false.


Converge operates from a different premise. The path to a lasting partnership is not more access — it is a better process. Structure does not impede genuine connection; it creates the conditions under which genuine connection is more likely to occur.


This platform was not built to scale for everyone. It was built for those who have already decided what they want, and who are prepared to pursue it with the seriousness it deserves.

Common Questions

Asked and answered.

Is this a dating app?

No. Converge is a structured courtship system. There is no app to download, no algorithm to game, no feed to scroll. Introductions are curated, not generated by automated matching. The distinction matters.

Why limit connections to three?

Because divided attention produces diluted investment. Three is not an arbitrary number — it is enough to allow genuine evaluation without encouraging the shallow parallelism that characterizes most dating platforms. The limit enforces seriousness, not scarcity.

Why audio-only for the first two calls?

Visual presentation triggers immediate, often unconscious judgements that short-circuit genuine assessment. Audio-only calls require both parties to be present to the conversation itself. By the time video is available, members have already established a substantive foundation.

Why annual membership instead of monthly?

Monthly billing incentivizes platforms to manufacture urgency and extend engagement. An annual commitment aligns the platform's incentives with yours: we succeed when you find what you came for, not by keeping you subscribed indefinitely.

Is Converge inclusive?

Converge is open to all adults, regardless of gender, orientation, ethnicity, religion, or background, who are seeking a serious long-term partnership or marriage. The one non-negotiable is intent.

What if I do not wish to continue with someone?

You communicate that directly through the platform. Converge provides a clear, structured closing process. Ghosting is not acceptable conduct. Members are expected to treat one another with the same respect they would want extended to themselves.

Why so structured?

Because structure, applied well, is not constraint — it is clarity. The framework exists to reduce the anxiety of open-ended ambiguity and to create shared expectations between people who are, after all, strangers attempting something significant together.

Is relocation required to participate?

No. Converge operates globally. Geographic compatibility is a factor considered during the introduction process, but the early stages of courtship proceed remotely by design. Where and whether relocation is relevant is a conversation that belongs to the people involved, not the platform.
